## On variety names

Hop variety names such as Citra®, Mosaic®, Simcoe®, Nelson Sauvin™ and Aramis™
are trademarks or registered trademarks of their respective owners. Several
varieties are additionally protected by plant patents or plant breeders' rights,
recorded in each record's `ownership` block where known.

These names are used here nominatively — to identify which plant a record
describes. That is what a variety name is for. No claim of ownership or
affiliation is made or implied, and no record here grants any right to
propagate, sell or reproduce a protected variety.

## On the data

Measured physical and chemical properties of a plant — alpha acid content, oil
composition, pedigree, year of release — are facts. Facts are not copyrightable.
Recording them, citing where each figure came from, and publishing that record
is what this project does.

What *is* protected is another party's particular compilation, selection and
arrangement of those facts. HopLore does not reproduce anyone's database. Where
a figure came from a secondary compilation rather than a sheet read directly,
that compilation is cited by name in `data/sources.yml` with `tier: aggregator`,
and the citation is visible on the rendered page.

If you are a rights holder and believe something here oversteps, open an issue
or email the maintainer and it will be dealt with promptly and without argument.

## On accuracy

Hops are an agricultural product. Alpha acid varies by crop year, field, harvest
timing and storage. Every figure here is a record of what a source published,
not a guarantee about the sack in your freezer. Brew accordingly, and always
trust the analysis printed on your actual lot over anything on this site.
