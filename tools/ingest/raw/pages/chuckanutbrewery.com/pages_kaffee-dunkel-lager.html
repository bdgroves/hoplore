Kaffee Dunkel Lager

Kaffee Dunkel Lager

ABV: 5%
IBU’S: 22

Tasting Notes

Dark mahogany, malty, Ethiopian coffee, moderate bitterness

Description

Our classic Dunkel is a dark mahogany Munchener Lager with balanced, smooth toffee & chocolate notes. In this variation we have partnered with our friends at Bellingham’s Steed Coffee Companyu using their Huehuetenango Guatemalan beans to add another smooth layer of chocolate and coffee notes to our traditional Dunkel.

Food Pairing

Wonderful with desserts or just a pick me up drink in the afternoon!
