FRESH HOP SEASON IS HERE!

GRAB YOURSELF A FRESH HOP IOWA IPA

The Iowa, a beloved one off, has returned in the fresh hop form. Why wouldn’t ya? Loaded with Citra, Mosaic, Simcoe, and a hefty firstful of fresh Strata hops from Crosby Farms in Woodburn, OR. It’s a sticky, resinous, fun brawl of citrus, resinous tropics, and a hint of cannabis.

  • Type

    Fresh Hop IPA

  • ABV

    6.4%

  • IBU

    65

  • Yeast

    Kölsch

  • Hops

    Simcoe, Citra and Mosaic and fresh hop Strata in the kettle, a Fresh Hop Strata hop back, and Citra, Mosaic, and Simcoe and Strata CGX in the dry hop

  • Malt

    Gambrinus Pilsner, Weyermann Carafoam, Carahell, and Acidulated

  • Appearance

    Iowa Corn Field

  • Aroma

    Tangelo, stone fruit, fresh cut grass, grapefruit rind, cannabis, Mineola orange, diesel

  • Mouth

    Smoother than the aroma would suggest, but not pillowy, tangerine, ripe citrus pith, mellow tropics, ganja, melon

  • Finish

    More bitter upfront than it finishes, tropic citrus, dank citrus resin, cannabis

  • Fresh Hops

    Strata from Crosby Farms

Brewer's Notes

Well, it’s back again!  That one beer we thought we’d only make 40 barrels.  But dammit, it’s good.  So here we are making it again!  

 

We kept things essentially the same as last year, and unlike the other fresh hop beers we brew, this one is a straight translation of the original recipe, except we chuck a whole bunch of fresh hops in the hop back.  The Strata we got from Crosby this year did not disappoint.  This variety comes in a small cone, but packs a big punch.  They’re sticky, highly aromatic, and just lovely.  

 

It comes across as a nice example of the differences and similarities of wet hops.  To us, it comes across as a little more rounded and less sharp.  Still delicious and awesome, just more complex and smooth.  The malt base for this beer allows the hops to shine, and the combination of CGX, T90, and Fresh Hop Whole Cone really balances out well.
