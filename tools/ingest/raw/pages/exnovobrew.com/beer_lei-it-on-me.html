Lei It On Me

Lei It On Me

Bright, Tropical, Fruity, Refreshing

Tropical, juicy, and smooth—this beer brings sunshine in a glass. A crisp pale base is rounded out with malted wheat and flaked oats for a soft, full mouthfeel. Re-fermented on heaps of passionfruit, orange, and guava purée, it bursts with bright, tangy fruit flavors and a lush, tropical finish.
