Fresh Hop Beers Every Friday
Fresh hop season is a sacred time in Pacific Northwest brewing. To mark the harvest, we’re hosting Fresh Fridays all September long at our Ballard Taproom! Every Friday, we’ll be pouring newly tapped fresh hop beers accompanied by live music from local musicians 🍻
