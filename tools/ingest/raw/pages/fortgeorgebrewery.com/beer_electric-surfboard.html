Electric Surfboard

9% ABV

Extreme sports are great. But how could they be better? Why, electricity and money of course. If you add electricity and money to any extreme sport, suddenly you have an extreme luxury sport. The same goes for Double IPAs. Add an electrifying hop bill, a lot of expensive time in the tank, and suddenly – bam – an extreme luxury Double IPA in an electric blue 4-pack. Electric Surfboard Double IPA – available across the PNW all year long. 

Brewer's Notes

Can art by Larry Shuler of Shuler Surfboards.

Hops: Apollo, Ekuanot, Mosaic, Simcoe
Malts: Rahr Pilsner, Grain Miller’s Flaked Wheat, Briess Carapils
Yeast: Kaiser (German Alt)
