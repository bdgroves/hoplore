WHO WE ARE

At Ghostfish Brewing Company, we’re reinventing beer from the ground up. We love to craft gluten free beers that pay tribute to classic styles but also expand your expectations of what gluten free beer can be. It all starts with the ingredients. With the utmost care, we hand select naturally gluten free grains, yeast strains, and Pacific Northwest hops that lead to boundary pushing craft beers that delight beer lovers near and far. Explore our site and find a treasure trove of year round, seasonal, and limited beer releases you won’t want to miss. Keep updated on our Instagram for seasonal beer releases parties, special collaborations, and taproom events!
