Gose-style Sour Ale brewed with grapefruit puree & San Juan Island Sea Salt. Tart, Fruity, & Refreshing. OUT NOW

Fresh, floral & vibrant on the nose with hints of citrus fruits, Yakima Valley Fresh Hop IPA expresses a perfect blend of hop character that is both bold & nuanced. Hops from Loza Farms in Yakima County: Centennial, Mt. Hood, Simcoe & Amarillo. OUT NOW

ABV: 6.2% IBU: N/A
