Our 2026 Yakima Valley Fresh Hop IPA is here

Deep golden in color and bursting with fresh from the field Yakima Valley hops. Floral and vibrant on the nose with hints of citrus fruits, Yakima Valley Fresh Hop IPA expresses a perfect blend of hop character that is both bold and nuanced. Fresh Amarillo hops from our friends at Loza Farms are added to the kettle within several hours of harvest.

Hops: Fresh Amarillo, Centennial
Malts: 2-Row, Munich, light crystal

6.2% ABV ~40 IBU
