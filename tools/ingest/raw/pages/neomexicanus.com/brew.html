    Learn How to Brew with Zappa® Hops and Unlock Bolder Flavors

    Named after Frank Zappa, in conjunction with the Zappa family, and released by CLS Farms, explore what makes this hop different from the others.

    AROMAS

    Evolving independently, Zappa® has developed its own unique and rebellious aroma and flavor characteristics.

    USAGE

    We recommend that Zappa® makes up less than 25% of the total hop bill to complement the other varieties. Combine with these hops to target specific flavors:

    Tropical Fruits: El Dorado®, Mosaic®, Strata®

    Red Fruits: Cashmere, Idaho Gem®

    Citrus: Centennial, Cascade, Chinook, Citra®

    01

    Kettle

    Zappa®'s bitterness contributions are low because of its 6-8% alpha acid range, but usage in the boil can result in more herbal and spice qualities. A late kettle addition is recommended when brewing Belgian-style ales like saisons or traditional lagers and American pale ales.

    02

    Whirlpool

    As a thiol-rich variety, using Zappa® in the whirlpool releases precursors to enhance tropical fruit flavors. Whirlpool usage is recommended when brewing hoppy-forward beers to maximize the full aroma spectrum. This is the part of the brewing process where Zappa® shines best.

    03

    Dry-Hop

    Bold and impulsive – Zappa® in the dry-hop should be complemented with other fruit-forward hop varieties. We suggest Zappa® makes up equal or less than 10% of the total dry-hop bill to enhance without overpowering the final beer.

    Craft Big Flavors with Less

    Neomexicanus™ hops are often described as polarizing. Knowing where and how to use a hop like Zappa® can mitigate undesired flavors and maximize on what makes this variety perfect for brewing a standout beer.

    Product Types
    Zappa® hops are available in traditional T-90 pellets and Abstrax Quantum Brite.
    Beer Styles

    Versatility and a wide aroma spectrum make Zappa® suitable for a range of beer styles. Brewers have found success in hazy pale ales, corn-based lagers, black ipas, wild fermentation beers, and saisons. Leverage Zappa®’s uniqueness to target specific flavors and give your consumers a new sensory experience.
