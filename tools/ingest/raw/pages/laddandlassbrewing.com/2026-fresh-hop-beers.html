—

Fresh Hop Krush Hazy IPA
Hazy IPA / 6.8% ABV
featuring Krush from Perrault Farms
—
One of the top hop breeders in the Yakima Valley, Perrault Farms is largely responsible for some of our favorite varieties (think Citra, Mosaic, Sabro*). That being said, one of our brewer’s favorite varieties from Perrault is the exceptionally-grown Krush. Our signature soft, juicy IPA base was infused with 200 pounds of fresh Krush hops. Post-fermentation, we boosted the tropical vibes with a bountiful dry hop of Cryo Krush, Cryo Citra, and Nectaron, one of our favorites from New Zealand.

*Yes, L&L is pro-Sabro.

Fresh Hop Pale Ale
Hazy IPA / 5.5% ABV
featuring Chinook from Roy Farms
—
Andy Roy of Roy Farms (WA) was one of the first hop farmers to take notice of the first-rate beers we’re brewing at L&L, which led to our collaborating on Shifting Latitudes, a hazy pale ale brewed with Latitude 46’s experimental hop, ADHA 808. And we are pumped to continue our relationship with Roy Farms this season—brewing with 200 pounds of late-season Chinook.

Why late-season Chinook? Because the longer Chinook is allowed to hang on the bine, the brighter and more pineapple-forward it becomes. While Chinook has a reputation for piney dankness, late-season Chinook is a delightfully differently fruity beast of a hop. To amplify said fruitiness, we finished this pale ale with Cryo Citra and Cryo Columbus.
—
Hops harvested on: 20 SEP 2026
Beer brewed on: 20 SEP 2026
