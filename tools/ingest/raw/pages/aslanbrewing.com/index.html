NEW HAPPENINGS

  • Aslan Brewing X Community Food Co-op

    THE MAKING OF NORTH FOREST

    Aslan Brewing and the Community Food Co-op have been neighbors on North Forest Street for more than a decade. To commemorate our shared home, we teamed up to create North Forest — a limited-edition pilsner.

    Learn more

  • New Release: Das Boot

    FESTBIER

    Here at Aslan, we always welcome this festbier's return as it represents the transition from summer to fall.

    With a bit bigger body and ABV than a typical German lager, this festbier has us ready to celebrate the coming of fall and is perfect for the upcoming cooler nights!

    Learn more

  • New Release: Ginger Rye

    SPICED PALE ALE

    We're happy to see the return of an Aslan classic that has been with us from the beginning.

    Inspiration for this beer comes from a delicious cocktail called the "Moscow Moose."

    However you choose to enjoy Ginger Rye, may it be as unique and exciting as this beer. Cheers!

    Learn more

Aslan Brewing has been grounded in organic from the beginning, but we’re not stopping where we started. Organic is the essential ingredient in both our brews and our community. With these elevated agricultural standards in place, we are not only protecting the health of the crop and the raw ingredients with which we brew, but also the health of the farmers working in the fields, other non-target species, and the end consumer...YOU!
