We just started a brewery to meet more dogs.

Well behaved dogs are welcome any time, even inside. Kids are welcome until 8pm. Please do pay attention to them. We have treats at the bar for both!

We have light bar snacks and fresh hot pretzels, but you're welcome to bring your own food, and there are many take-out choices nearby.

We have Seattle's best taco truck (El Camion) practically on our doorstep. In addition to our great beer, we have five cider taps with local great cider, and a few Washington wines by the glass, for those in your party that aren't beer people.

We also have a variety of non alcoholic options, including soda, kombucha, and non alcoholic beer.
