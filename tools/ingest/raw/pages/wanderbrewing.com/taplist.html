Wander Litely American Lager

4.2% ABV // 10 IBU // 2 SRM

A carefully brewed American lager. With a stripped-down malt bill of pilsner malt and flaked barley, Wander Litely is light and dry with a slight graininess balanced by a whisper of floral and earthy hops.

Bronze – 2024 World Beer Cup
Gold – 2022 World Beer Cup
Gold – 2020 Great American Beer Festival

16oz Willi Becher & 60oz Pitchers // 12oz 6pk, Crowlers & Growlers to-go

Festbier

5.8% ABV // 20 IBU // 5 SRM

An evocative festbier experience, conjuring the peace and space of a sun bathed meadow. Sweet and malt forward while still refreshing and nimble. Featuring an exclusively German malt bill, as well as all German hops, showcasing in particular the quintessential Mittelfrüh noble hop. If you hold your ear up to the can you can practically hear yodeling.

Racoonalope Beer Steins Available!

16oz Oberglass // 16oz 4pk, Crowlers & Growlers to-go

Correspondent Export Stout

6.4% ABV // 40 IBU // 35 SRM

European specialty malts. American hops. House ale yeast. Crimson edged black. Smooth mouth feel. Roasted grain, sweet molasses, baker’s chocolate, subtle char.

Gold – 2024 Great American Beer Festival
Silver – 2022 World Beer Cup
Gold – 2019 Great American Beer Festival
Gold – 2017 Great American Beer Festival

16oz Willi Becher // 16oz 4pk, Growlers & Crowlers to-go

Raspberry Millie Sour

4.4% ABV // 5 IBU // 5 SRM

The latest offering of our rotating fruit sour ale. Kettle soured with lactobacillus before undergoing fermentation with fruit using our house ale strain. Colorful, crisp, dry, tart, raspberry.

16oz Tulip // 12oz 6pk, Growlers & Crowlers to-go

Shoe Toss IPA

6.8% ABV // 80 IBU // 9 SRM

Shoe Toss is a classic Pacific Northwest style India Pale Ale. It has the bite that made you fall in love with craft beer; but finishes with a crisp smack that demands you take a second sip. American malt. Pacific Northwest hops. House ale yeast. Glowing orange in color. Dry and crisp. Floral, citrus, grapefruit, spicy.

16oz IPA Glass // 12oz 6pk, Growlers & Crowlers to-go

Right Here Right Now Hazy IPA

6.2% ABV // 15 IBU // 6 SRM

RHRN is a super aromatic, easy drinking hazy IPA. Uncover the deepest depth of bright citrus and juicy pineapple in your expedition to the bottom of the glass. Wander around, enjoy the journey and live in the now!

16oz IPA Glass // 16oz 4pk, 12oz 6pk, Growlers & Crowlers to-go

Fresh Press West Coast Fresh Hop IPA

Roy Farms, Moxee, WA

6.5% ABV // 60 IBU // 6 SRM

A showcase of Citra, Amarillo, and Cascade hops. Clear and radiant canary colored. Transportational aroma that might as well be a cool breeze right off the hop fields themselves. Vivid hop character without being punishing. Vital and crisp. Featuring 300 lbs. of whole cone fresh hops from Roy Farms of Yakima Washington.

16oz IPA Glass // 16oz 4pk, Growlers & Crowlers to-go

Nectaron Single EyePA

6.4% ABV // 35 IBU // 4 SRM

A showcase of the emerging, New Zealand grown “Nectaron” hop. The only hop used in this one-off brew, we can confirm that Nectaron is as tropical and aromatically complex as it gets. We’re getting an aroma like a bowl of Froot Loops doused in fresh orange Julius. An extra smooth and rich drinking experience with barley and spelt malts acting as the springboard for the new peachy, pineapple-forward hops.

16oz IPA Glass // 16oz 4pk, Growlers & Crowlers to-go

Aussie Steve Pale Lager

4.9% ABV // 2 SRM

Brewed to celebrate Steve, local farmer and notable Australian who has picked up our spent grain to feed his cattle for so-long-we-don’t-even-remember-how-many years. A pale lager with Australian Galaxy hops that’s extra pale and extra crispy. Perfect for outdoor activities, and we like to think thirst quenching enough to stand up to a summer in Australia. Drinks quick and finishes dry with loads of white grape flavor and aroma. G’day mate!

16oz Oberglass // 12oz 6pk, Crowlers & Growlers to-go

Plum Bob

7.4% ABV

Refreshingly tart and fruity, Plum Bob pours a bright magenta hue to draw you in, then delivers a layered sour experience on the palate to reward your curiosity. Entering an oak foeder several years ago, back when times were simple, this effervescent fruity elixir eases your mind and reminds you that everything will be just fine.

12oz Stemmed Goblet // 16oz Single Cans & 16oz 4pks to-go

Woodinville Whiskey Bourbon Barrel-Aged Imperial Stout

Collaboration w/ Woodinville Whiskey, Woodinville, WA

10.1% ABV // 40 SRM

A rich imperial stout aged 10 months in freshly emptied Woodinville Bourbon Whiskey barrels. A massive grist consisting of our favorite 2-row North American barley alongside copious amounts of English kilned roasted barley, chocolate malt, and double roasted crystal. Immediately you can smell the oak barrel wood before diving deeper into apricot, dried cranberry and heaps of baker’s chocolate to go with the unmistakable bourbon character. Color of the darkest black coffee. A truly decadent sipper.

12oz Stemmed Goblet // Growlers & Crowlers to-go

Range Life IPA

6.0% ABV // 45 IBU // 5 SRM

A modern West-Coast IPA featuring a tropical cocktail of the juiciest Yakima Valley hops. Barley and spelt malt round out the grist for this uniquely PNW brew– juicy with just enough snapping bitterness to lure you into the vastness of an exotic, but welcoming Range.

16oz IPA Glass // 16oz 4pk, Growlers & Crowlers to-go
