Hazy IPA Rotator Series

Hazy India Pale Ale

  • ABV: 6.5%
  • IBU: 45

Lunar Phases Hazy IPA

Lunar Phases Hazy IPA moves with the rhythm of the night sky. With a generous hopping of Citra®, Krush™, and Dolcita™ hops, this Hazy IPA glows with aromas of tropical fruit and citrus that shift and shimmer with every sip. Juicy, smooth, and beautifully balanced, it’s brewed for every mood, every moment, and every phase. Crack one open, look up, and let the haze wax on.

Flavor Profile:

Aromas of tropical fruit and citrus

Ingredients:

Malt: Pilsner, White Wheat, Flaked Oats

Hops: Citra®, Krush™, and Dolcita™
