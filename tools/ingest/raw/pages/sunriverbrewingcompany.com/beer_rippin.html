Rippin

NW Pale Ale

6%
ABV

Rip into the Northwest’s most awarded pale ale.  Hefty whirlpool additions and a bold double dry hop of hand selected hops make this American-style pale ale burst with ripe tropical fruit, freshly squeezed citrus and resinous pine.  The finish is distinctly hoppy, both dank and fruity, with a restrained bitterness.

Hops:
Citra, Citra Cryo, Mosaic, Mosaic Cryo, Amarillo®, Amarilo® CGX

Stomp

Stomp

west coast IPA

6.6
ABV

This modern IPA is lean, mean, and precise, stomping on traditions, and blazing a new trail. A skinny malt bill of Idaho-grown pale barley creates a clean, lightly sweet base for a blend of hand selected PNW hops. Mosaic, Citra, and Krush Cryo hops deliver reliably tropical and citrus notes, while the Columbus Cryo hits with supporting diesel, woodsy, and berry character

Vicious

Vicious

India Pale Ale

7%
ABV

Welcome to Sunriver Brewing Company’s flagship beer – Vicious Mosquito IPA.  This buzz worthy brew has all the classic character that you’d expect from an American-style IPA.  A hearty blend of Idaho-grown barley creates a cracker forward malt character, while bold hopping rates deliver a vicious punch of citrus zest, woodsy pine and a hint of red berries. Well balanced and with a true-to-style bitterness, this reliably hoppy brew finishes clean and dry.

Centennial, Columbus, Columbus Cryo, Simcoe Cryo

Fuzztail

Fuzztail

Hefeweizen

5%
ABV

Our gold medal-winning American style hefeweizen is the perfect partner for after work or after play.  Idaho-grown pale and wheat malts create a gentle sweetness with bready notes.  A blend of hand-selected PNW hops gives this hazy, refreshing wheat beer a crisp finish with pleasant notes of grapefruit, lemon, and evergreen. Coolwater

Coolwater

Lager

4.5%
ABV

This cool, crispy, small batch, handmade beer defines the light lager style.  Coolwater is born in the Upper Deschutes watershed and brewed with premium malted barley, flaked corn, and a kiss of hops.   Extended cold conditioning creates a brilliant straw colored, light bodied lager that is 100% crushable.

Cocoa Cow

Cocoa Cow

Chocolate Milk Stout

7%
ABV

Moo for a brew and get into this udderly delicious dark ale.  Pale and caramel malts combine with milk sugar to create the rich, full body of the PNW’s most award-winning stout.  Light roasted notes of cocoa and fudge come from three varieties of imported chocolate malt.  Each batch of Cocoa Cow is conditioned on 15 pounds of Ghana-grown cacao nibs.  Have some dessert today – just make sure it’s served in a glass!
