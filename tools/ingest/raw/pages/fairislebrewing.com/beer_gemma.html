Gemma

a saison with foraged elderflower and rose hips

Herbaceous and botanical, Gemma is a precious saison infused with rose hips and dried elderflowers foraged from the Cascades by Andrew’s long-time friend and ethnobotanist, Alex. Batch two has a more pronounced floral and elderflower aroma followed by tart undertones from the rose hips.
