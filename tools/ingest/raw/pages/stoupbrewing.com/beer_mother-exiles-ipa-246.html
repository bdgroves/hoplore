This beer is inspired by American poet Emma Lazarus who wrote MOTHER OF EXILES in 1883 to raise money for the construction of the pedestal of the Statue of Liberty. It was originally brewed as part of the #beertrumpshate campaign but it is clear that immigrant and refugee crises can happen anytime and anywhere. Every time this beer comes into our rotation it will highlight a community in need and proceeds will be donated to an organization addressing those needs. This year we are supporting Project Feast, an organization that benefits refugees and immigrants by providing culinary training that equips them for careers or entrepreneurship in the food industry.<\/div><\/div>
