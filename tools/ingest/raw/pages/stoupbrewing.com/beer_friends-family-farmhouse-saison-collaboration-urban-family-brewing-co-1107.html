Urban Family's Friends & Family Series is a selection of fun barrel aged projects dreamt up by them and some of their brewing friends .... like us!! Each is unique, and captures the spirit of two breweries coming together to make fun things for you to share with someone you like.<\/span><\/p>\r\n

First in this new line of collaborations, is a mixed-culture farmhouse Saison. This beer was brewed at UF's Magnolia brewhouse in early 2019 with the intention of letting it age to perfection until they moved in across the street. The malt bill was loaded with Bohemian Floor-Malted Pilsner, wheat, oats and a dash of aromatic malt. The beer was initially fermented with Saison DuPont yeast, and was then racked into red wine barrels from Efeste with a blend of mixed cultures. After 10 months, this beauty was ready to head into bottles where it slowly bottle conditioned for another few months.<\/span><\/p>\r\n

This saison has a funky aroma with classic Belgian esters and a touch of red wine and oak. With a mild acidity this beer is easy drinking and an excellent example of what you can do when you’re inspired by your friends.<\/span><\/p>\r\n

Until we can drink a beer with our friends again, we settle for enjoying beers we made with them.<\/span><\/p><\/div>
