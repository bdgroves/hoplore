The women of Stoup came together to brew a Cold IPA for this year's Pink Boots Collaboration Brew. A base of Pilsner malt with flaked rice sets the stage with a light, crisp base. Warrior Extract for bittering and 2020 Pink Boots Hop Blend in the whirlpool keeps the bittering moderate. We fermented this beer with our house lager yeast, but at higher fermentation temperatures, then dry hopped with the 2021 Yakima Chief Hops Pink Boots Blend (HBC 630, Idaho Gem, Loral, Talus, Triumph), Bru-1, Eclipse and Citra Spectrum for an big fruity nose. The result is a light, crisp IPA with intense aromas of tropical fruit, citrus and pineapple. A portion of proceeds will be donated to our local Pink Boots Chapter to support education for womxn in the fermented beverage industry.<\/div><\/div>
