When Brewer Andy asked if he could whip up a beer to share with his family, he led with \"it's nothing crazy\". He then proceeded to load it up with all sorts of crazy. Crazy delicious! This IPA is built on a base of 2-row, Vienna and Wheat Malts. We then added El Dorado and Cryo Pop in the kettle, Citra Spectrum during fermentation and  HBC 586, Idaho 7 Cryo, Ekuanot, and Loral Cryo in the dry hop.
