We brewed this beer in collaboration with the Washington Brewers Guild and 21 other breweries. The Guild split the state into 5 regions and we each brewed a West Coast IPA. This beer represents the Puget Sound region. We started with a base of 2-row, Pilsner and Wheat malt for a light, crisp body. Then added Citra Dynaboost, a new flowable product from YCH and HBC 586 in the whirlpool. We dry hopped with HBC 586 Cryo, Citra Cryo and HBC 1019.
