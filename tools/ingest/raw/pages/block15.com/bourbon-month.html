Bourbon Month

Held every February, Bourbon Month is Block 15’s celebration of all things bourbon, including the release of our Super Nebula bourbon barrel-aged beer series, special events, a stamp book of specialty bourbons to try, and food and drink offerings.

A Celebration of All Things Bourbon

Beginning January 30th, 2026, and running throughout February, we host Bourbon Month events at our Downtown Pub and Southtown Tap Room, with unique offerings at Caves Corvallis.

We celebrate the release of our bourbon barrel-aged Super Nebula Series with curated food specials crafted by our Executive Chef, Sarah, specialty bourbon cocktails by our front-of-house managers, and a chance to try a selection of specialty bourbons.

Bourbon Month also includes a more exclusive series of ticketed events, such as the Brewer’s Dinners and Guided Pairings at Block 15 locations and a Bourbon Dinner hosted at Caves Corvallis.

Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when tickets go on sale!

2026’s Bourbon Barrel-Aged Beers

  • Super Nebula

    Imperial Stout Matured in Bourbon Barrels with Cocoa Nibs • 13.80%

    This year's Super Nebula was matured in Willett bourbon barrels and then conditioned with wild-harvested, fair-trade cocoa nibs, carefully selected to complement its decadent character.

  • Super Nebula Valhalla

    Imperial Stout Matured in Bourbon Barrels with Cocoa Nibs, Cardamom, Star Anise, and Juniper • 13.80%

    From our cellars, Valhalla features fair-trade chocolate, cardamom, star anise, and juniper. This bold stout, aged in Willett Bourbon barrels, delivers intense cocoa and spice. It's an opulent brew that effortlessly conquers the North wind.

  • Kriek

    Oud Bruin Matured in Bourbon Barrels with Cherries • 7.40%

    Spontaneously fermented in our wild cellar, Kriek is our Oud Bruin matured over two winters on locally farmed tart Montmorency cherries in second-run bourbon barrels, first used to mature Super Nebula, our Imperial Stout.

Taste Through a Curated List
of Bourbons at Our Locations

Beginning January 30th, 2026, and running throughout February, Block 15 features a selection of specialty bourbons and whiskeys at our Downtown Pub and our sister restaurant, Caves Corvallis. This year’s list of Bourbons was curated in collaboration with Alex Goodwin, former manager at Block 15.

Stamp Books

Each year, we offer a Stamp Book to track which bourbons and beers you’ve savored at our Corvallis locations. For those who complete at least 13 of the 17 offerings, we hold a party later in the year and award you with a special piece of merch. Get your Stamp Book at any location!

Completed Stamp Books must be turned in at any location by the end of the day on Saturday, February 28th, to be considered for the prizes.

This year’s party for those who complete the book will be held on April 8th.
