Synergy Series

Collaborations between Block 15 Brewing and friends.

Featured Collaboration:
Buzzworks

West Coast IPA • 6.25%
Collaboration with Chris King Precision Components

A precision-crafted celebration of Chris King's 50th year of legendary attention to detail. Simcoe, Cashmere, and Anchovy hops impart bright citrus and stone fruit notes, with a crisp malt backbone for a dialed-in IPA experience.

Designed to pack maximum flavor without the extra weight, this beer was designed with our friends in the biking community in mind, but is also perfect for anybody wanting to push through their day without compromising on character.

Buzzworks will be first available at Chris King’s Open House event on August 20th at their spot in NW Portland. It will be available at Block 15 locations starting August 21st, and around the PNW the following week.

Featured Collaboration:
Septembeerfest

American Wheat Ale • 5.25%
Collaboration with Calapooia, Sky High, Oregon Trail, the Heart of the Valley Homebrewers Club, and celebrity brewing icon Dave Marliave

An American Wheat brewed with the most influential minds in Willamette Valley craft brewing. Featuring the creative minds at Block 15, Calapooia Brewing, Sky High Brewing, Oregon Trail Brewing, the Heart of the Valley Homebrewers Club, and celebrity brewing icon Dave Marliave. Simcoe, Motueka, and ground coriander shine in this fresh take on an American classic.

Brewed to raise awareness for Septembeerfest - the Willamette Valley's most enjoyable beer fest - where you can always find good food, good drinks, a shady spot in the grass, and great company. Septembeerfest is held August 29th, 2026, at Avery Park in Corvallis.

Septembeerfest will be available on draft only at Block 15 locations starting Friday, August 14th, at select collaborators’ locations around Corvallis the following week, and at the festival on August 29th.

Featured Collaboration:
Banker’s Bills

Hazy IPA • 5.50%
Collaboration with Von Ebert Brewing

We pushed this collaboration with our friends at Von Ebert Brewing in Portland as far into POG territory as possible. This Fluffhead riff banks tropical Southern Hemisphere hops with groundbreaking thin-film Apollo extract.

A lower ABV version of Fluffhead that doesn't compromise on flavor. The first beer you've ever tried using brand-new Hopsteiner thin-film Apollo extract.

Banker’s Bills will be available at Block 15 locations starting July 24th, and around the PNW the following week.

Special Projects

  • Garden on the Block

    Tropical IPA • 6.00%
    July 2026

    A white-hot tropical dream machine made to help celebrate beergarden’s annual Beach Bash. Flooded with the most exotic fruit-forward hops and brewed for our friends at beergarden in Eugene, this one will have you reaching for your swimsuit and sandals.

    Our take on the Tropical IPA features Anchovy, Nectaron, and Sultana Lupulin Pellets.

  • Fuji to Hood 2026

    Japanese Rice Lager • 4.80%
    July 2026

    Aka Fuji Sake of You was brewed as part of Fuji to Hood, an event that pairs brewers from Oregon and Japan in collaboration each year. We are honored to be paired with Y. Market Brewing once again, and are excited to share another beer with their brewers soon.

    Y. Market Brewing is based in Nagoya, Japan. We brewed this draft-only beer with Akakome, a heritage Japanese rice, Koji culture, and co-fermented with Sake and lager yeast.

  • A Study of Late-Harvest Hops

    PNW Pale Ale • 5.69%
    May 2026

    Made as part of a statewide collaboration hosted by Celebrate Oregon Beer, Oregon Homegrown, which features Oregon's best breweries. Devil’s Staircase was brewed with Crosby’s Deep Cut Cascade hops to showcase the best that Oregon has to offer and to explore the late harvest's effect on this familiar hop. Expect classic Cascade hop character, plus extra-juicy tropical aroma.

  • A Celebration of Independence

    PNW IPA • 6.50%
    April 2026

    Brewed in collaboration with our friends at Xicha Brewing and Hetty Alice Beer, this beer is a herald of good things to come in the truly self-reliant craft brewing community. Each brewery made three distinct beers that reflect its identity. We made a PNW IPA with an uber exciting array of hops, including Anchovy, Columbus Cryo, and Sultana Lupulin pellets.

    Hetty Alice, distributed by Day One Distribution, made a West Coast IPA. Xicha, distributed by Upstanding Beer and Cider, made a Hazy IPA.

  • Carrying on Tradition with Joel Rea

    Oyster Stout • 4.50%
    March 2026

    Every year on Valentine's Day, Joel from Corvallis Brewing Supply would brew an oyster stout to be drunk on St. Patrick's Day. Sadly, they closed their doors in 2022. To keep the world from being deprived of this most festive tradition, we offered to brew it with him at our pub brewery! A dry Irish stout brewed with whole oysters in the mash and shells in the boil, Block Pearl has a unique mouthfeel and overall experience.

  • A Fresh Hop for The BeerMongers 16th Anniversary

    Fresh Hop Belgian Pale Ale • 7.30%
    September 2025

    We made Tuck’s Harvest in celebration of our friends The BeerMongers' 16th anniversary, drawing inspiration from Orval. We fermented this beer in our traditional coolship and dry-hopped it with Oregon-grown Hallertau Mittelfrüh fresh hops from Indie Hops, allowing it to remain unique despite its inspiration from nearly a thousand years of brewing tradition.

  • Loyal Legion Turns 10

    West Coast IPA • 6.85%
    August 2025

    Loyal to the Block is a small-batch West Coast IPA made in collaboration with Loyal Legion. It uses Nectaron, El Dorado, Citra, and Simcoe hops and is generously dry-hopped with Nectaron, Citra, and El Dorado. Full of bright citrus notes, this beer is perfect for toasting.

  • A Celebration of Brotherly Love with Dry Town Tap Station

    Hazy IPA • 6.90%
    July 2025

    This brew was developed to capture the spirit of brotherly love between Blake, Block 15’s Oregon Chain Manager, and Chad, Bar Manager at Dry Town. Made with a wealth of advanced hop products, it bursts with citrus and tropical flavors, complemented by the creamy, round mouthfeel of the hazy IPA we all know and love.

  • A Brew to Celebrate 9 Years of Loyal Legion

    West Coast IPA • 7.00%
    July 2024

    This small-batch beer was made with the hop lover in mind. We used Nectaron, El Dorado, Citra, and Simcoe hops and generously dry-hopped it with Nectaron, Citra, and El Dorado. Full of bright citrus notes, this beer was perfect for toasting at Loyal Legion’s tiki-themed 9th-anniversary celebration in Southeast Portland.

  • A Fully Loaded IPA for CBC with Our Pals at Crosby

    West Coast IPA • 6.80%
    April 2024

    We brewed this small-batch beer with the Crosby crew to highlight our respective operations, utilizing a litany of their hop products to create something we’re truly proud of that will be showcased at the Craft Brewers Conference. B&D Farms’ Cascade, Crosby Estate Grown Comet CGX, and New Zealand-Grown Pacific Sunrise hops combine to create an IPA bursting with tropical fruit character, pine, grapefruit, and orange zest.

  • Celebrating On Tap Bend’s 6th Anniversary

    American IPA • 6.50%
    April 2024

    Erik & Laura brewed this beer at Block 15’s Downtown Pub to serve at their 6th Anniversary party in Bend, Oregon. This special brew balanced a light malt body with a delightful blend of tangerine, grapefruit, citrus zest, pine, and tropical fruit notes. We added a special mix of CTZ, Chinook, Mosaic, and Apollo CO2 extract to the boil, and for the double dry-hop, we’ve included Mosaic, Cashmere, and Chinook.

  • A Dedication to 5 Years

    Strong Wild Red Ale Matured in Port Barrels • 11.00%
    January 2024

    Brewed and barreled in Spring 2021, Green Light is a celebration of Wildwood Taphouse's 5th Anniversary. Brewed with wild yeast and aged in port barrels, this strong Red Ale delivers bright acidity, ample barrel character, and a clean finish. Will and Andrew helped bottle the beer with us, leading up to the release.
