Mandarina Bavaria

Mandarina Bavaria is a German aroma hop known for its vibrant tangerine and sweet citrus character. Developed as part of Germany’s effort to breed new-school varieties, it brings juicy notes of mandarin orange, lime, and subtle tropical fruit, layered with a soft floral and herbal background. With moderate alpha acids and expressive aromatics, it’s perfect for dry hopping or late additions in modern IPAs, pale ales, and wheat beers.
