Cascade

CAS

United States

Cascade helped pioneer the modern pale ale, walking so that popular IPA hops like Citra could run. Bred from Fuggles and Serebrianker, Cascade was developed in the USA for a breeding program in Oregon and released in 1972. Thanks to its high levels of myrcene, they have an intense citrussy flavor that goes down easy. Making up 10% of all hops grown in the United States, Cascade is still the definitive choice for American Pale Ales and Blonde Ales.

  • Aroma Profile

Typical Aroma Profile

Pink Grapefruit combined with passion fruit, mango and currants show Cascade with an Australian complexity.
